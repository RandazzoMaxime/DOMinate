# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in DOMinate a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, doxxing and
publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile.
All reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly. Maintainers may edit,
remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions, or
temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is inappropriate.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
